COMPLETE STRUCTURAL PROOF FOR THE ORIGINAL CODEGREE-FOUR CONFIGURATIONS

Status: coordinator consolidation of the independently reviewed component
proofs. All eleven four-vertex core types are accounted for structurally.
No upstream census, saved-certificate completeness, or solver result is a
premise. This is not a claim of novelty or formal proof-assistant verification.

1. PRECISE LOCAL THEOREM.

Let G be a finite simple graph, and let u,v be adjacent vertices of degree
seven whose common neighborhood C has exactly four vertices. Write
  N(u)={v} union C union A,
  N(v)={u} union C union B,
where A,B are disjoint two-sets, and put H=G[C]. Assume
  (A1) G[N(u)] and G[N(v)] are connected and not WKE;
  (A2) deg_H(x)+|N(x) intersect A|+|N(x) intersect B|<=5 for every x in C.

A graph F is WKE if some matching M and vertex set Q satisfy |Q|<=|M|
and every edge of F with both endpoints outside Q belongs to M.

Then there exist an edge-disjoint triangle packing S in G and an edge set
X subset E(G) satisfying
  |X|<=2|S|;
  X meets every triangle containing u or v;
  X contains every packed edge whose endpoints both avoid {u,v}.
Thus {u,v} is a reducible set in Puleo's sense. Arbitrary edges between A
and B and arbitrary edges outside these full hub neighborhoods are permitted.
No additional bound on ambient degrees of private vertices is assumed.

2. EXHAUSTION OF CORE TYPES WITHOUT A GRAPH CENSUS.

The core has four vertices and between zero and six edges. At zero or one
edge its type is unique. Two edges are adjacent (P3 plus isolate) or
disjoint (2K2). At three edges, a disconnected core must be triangle plus
isolate; a connected core is a four-vertex tree, either P4 or K1,3. At four
edges its two missing edges are disjoint (giving C4) or adjacent (giving
paw). Five edges give diamond, and six give K4. These are all possibilities.

For the empty and single-edge types, each connected seven-vertex link is
WKE by the explicit witnesses and the stated use of Puleo Corollary 4.12 in
  compression/round3/E/zero_core_exclusions.txt.
They contradict (A1) and cannot occur.

For every other type, the following reviewed proof derives a complete
structural disjunction from (A1),(A2) and supplies literal S,X for each
branch. The file paths are relative to the repository root.

  Core                   Proof file
  P3 plus isolate        compression/round3/E/sparse_star_cores.txt
  2K2                    compression/theory/human_case_2k2.txt
  K1,3                   compression/round3/E/sparse_star_cores.txt
  P4                     compression/round3/E/path_core.txt
  Triangle plus isolate  research/round8/E/triangle_isolate_core.txt
  Paw                    research/round8/E/paw_core.txt
  C4                     research/round7/E/c4_core.txt
  Diamond                research/round6/E/diamond_core.txt
  K4                     compression/round3/D/k4_human_proof.txt

Each construction checks triangle existence, edge-disjointness, X subset E,
the factor-two budget, every ambient hub triangle, and containment of packed
external edges. Since the core types above exhaust all four-vertex graphs,
and the two exceptional types are impossible, one of those constructions
always applies. This proves the local theorem.

3. WHY THESE CERTIFICATES GIVE A REDUCTION.

Put G'=(G-X)-{u,v}. If G' satisfies tau(G')<=2nu(G'), take a maximum packing
P and minimum cover Y there. Every S-edge either meets {u,v} or is in X,
so P and S are edge-disjoint. Also X union Y covers G: hub triangles meet X;
all other triangles either meet X or survive in G'. Hence
  tau(G)<=|X|+|Y|<=2|S|+2|P|<=2nu(G).
This is the ambient induction bridge; checking only a standalone local
graph would not have been sufficient.

5. WHAT IS AND IS NOT COMPLETE.

This completes a human-readable structural explanation of reducibility for
the original A1+A2 codegree-four configurations. Gupta had already proved
the corresponding original local proposition through a finite certificate
catalogue. The starting source and data attribution remain
  Anish Gupta, "Tuza's conjecture for graphs of maximum degree at most seven,"
  arXiv:2608.06538v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06538v1
and its pinned companion repository/data (see compression/input/PROVENANCE.txt).
The WKE framework and the named imported theorem above are due to
  Gregory J. Puleo, arXiv:1308.2211v3,
  https://arxiv.org/html/1308.2211v3 .
No literature-priority conclusion is drawn for the component constructions.

Separately, nine signed templates cover the 1,144 supplied records and are
optimal in the fixed dictionary of 499 catalogue-derived pattern orbits.
That exact finite-dictionary result remains proved by its independent
upper/lower-certificate checks. The structural argument here does NOT claim
that every A1+A2 configuration must match one of those same nine patterns;
it may use other explicitly proved recipes. Nor does either result prove
a global minimum over arbitrary structural-template languages.

Finally, (A2) holds in the original maximum-degree-seven setting because
each common vertex is also incident to u and v. It does not follow merely
from mad(G)<8. The present local theorem therefore does not settle that
separate maximum-average-degree question or replace the required low-degree
joint counting argument.
