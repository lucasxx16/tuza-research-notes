Round 3 C: adversarial scope audit

The nine signed templates each give a universally valid packing and deletion
whenever its signed rim conditions hold. The independent reconstruction and
exact nine-record lower bound establish nine as optimal only among the fixed
499 catalogue-derived pattern orbits on the 1,144 supplied records. They do
not prove that the nine patterns cover every admissible graph without the
upstream census. The 2K2-core case has a separate human argument. The eight
other nonempty core types still need a structural implication from connected
non-WKE links and the common-vertex degree budget to at least one sufficient
template, or a different direct certificate construction. A recipe theorem or
normal form proves sufficiency or conditional conversion, not existence.

A global minimum must fix the universe (supplied records or all admissible
local graphs), template grammar and allowed parameters, equivalence under
relabeling, applicability semantics, and objective (number of templates,
conditions, or witness types). Unrestricted named recipes can encode an
arbitrary disjunction as one template, so an unqualified global minimum is
undefined. A lower bound for one finite dictionary cannot transfer to a
larger grammar without proving that every permitted template hits at most
one of the lower-bound configurations.
