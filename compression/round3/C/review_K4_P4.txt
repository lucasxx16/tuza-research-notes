Round 3 C adversarial review of saved K4 and P4 proofs

I reviewed D/k4_human_proof.txt line by line and accept it. Lemma 1's WKE
matchings and K5 obstruction are correct; the degree budget then makes each
core vertex have exactly one private neighbor on each side. Lemma 2 exhausts
all pairs of binary partitions. In generic case G, the six triangles use
distinct spokes and seven distinct external edges. The five other cover
edges meet uv-core triangles, the sole uncovered K4 rim xw, both private
fibers, and possible private-pair edges. A selected fiber may be empty;
the packed z-alpha(z) or y-beta(y) rim is still valid. In exceptional case E,
d=a when aa' exists and d=w otherwise makes a'd present and preserves
edge-disjointness. Its five spoke cover edges handle all four uv-core
triangles, the three omitted K4 rims, A/B attachments, and optional bb'.
Both covers have twelve edges and contain every external packing edge.

I also reviewed E/path_core.txt and accept it. Lemma M gives explicit
three-edge WKE matchings in every case when attachment support has size
at most two. The P4 distance-two involution forces opposite-side attached
vertices, and reversal or hub exchange gives Lemma D's form. Lemma D's
five-triangle packing and ten-edge cover satisfy all reduction conditions
in the ambient graph. The degree budget is not used in this P4 proof.

These are complete structural proofs for their stated core types, not a
proof that the listed core types exhaust admissible configurations. E's
later zero_core_exclusions.txt supplies a structural exclusion of empty
and single-edge cores using Puleo's Corollary 4.12 and explicit WKE
witnesses; I reviewed that saved text separately.
