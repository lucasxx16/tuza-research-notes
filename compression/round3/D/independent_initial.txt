Independent draft D, round 3.

Chosen direction: dense core K4. This draft is recorded before reading any
new mainline route or new templates. Sources read: verification/scope.txt,
upstream paper definitions and codegree-four statement, and the known
human_case_2k2.txt baseline.

Observation: Let a seven-vertex link consist of a K5 on {z} union C,
two exclusive vertices a,b, no edges za,zb, arbitrary C attachments,
and optional ab. Assume the link is connected. Then it is non-WKE iff
every core vertex has an attachment to {a,b}.

Necessity (contrapositive): if c has no attachment, choose Q=C\{c},
of size three. The induced graph outside Q on {z,c,a,b} has edge zc
and optional ab, with no other edge. If ab exists, the matching
{zc,ab,xy}, where xy is any edge inside Q, has size three and contains
all edges outside Q. If ab is absent, connectedness gives an attachment
a'x with x in Q; choose the other two Q vertices y,w and use matching
{zc,a'x,yw}. Again it contains all edges outside Q. This is WKE.

Sufficiency: every matching has size at most three. If Q has size at most
three and F-Q is a matching, the K5 forces Q to contain at least three of
its vertices, hence exactly three and no exclusive. Its two remaining
K5 vertices form an edge. One of these is a core vertex, and its attachment
to an exclusive makes degree at least two in F-Q, impossible.

Consequently, under the paired degree budget for H=K4, every core vertex
has exactly one A-neighbor and exactly one B-neighbor. Thus the attachment
graphs on each side are functions C -> A and C -> B. An empty fiber is
allowed only if the corresponding exclusive edge is present, by connectivity.

Next proof task: construct a uniform reducibility witness for two such
two-colorings of C and optional exclusive edges. The four attachment
types form a 2x2 multiplicity matrix of total four, up to row/column
permutations and transpose. This replaces arbitrary side masks by a small
human class whose cases may admit explicit witnesses.
