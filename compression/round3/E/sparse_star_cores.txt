HUMAN STRUCTURAL PROOFS FOR THE P3+ISOLATED AND K1,3 CORES

Status: census-free proofs. The proof uses explicit WKE witnesses for the
contrapositive and one five-triangle reducibility construction. No saved
certificate, side-mask list, or graph enumeration is a premise.

1. DEFINITIONS

A graph F is WKE if there are a matching M and a vertex set Q such that
|Q| <= |M| and every edge of F with both endpoints outside Q belongs
to M. Equivalently, Q covers F-M.

The local graph has adjacent hubs u,v, common set C of size four, and
exclusive pairs A={a0,a1}, B={b0,b1}. The links are the induced graphs
on N(u) and N(v). In either link write z for the opposite hub and
{a0,a1} for that link's private pair. Then z is adjacent to every core
vertex and to neither private vertex. Besides the core and z-spokes,
only core-private edges and the private edge a0a1 are possible.

The common-vertex degree budget is

  deg_H(c) + |N(c) intersect A| + |N(c) intersect B| <= 5

for each c in C, where H is the induced core graph.

2. A STAR LINK HAS AT LEAST TWO ATTACHED LEAVES

Lemma S. Suppose H is the star with center p and leaves q,r,s. If the
link is not WKE, at least two leaves have private neighbors.
Connectivity is unnecessary for this lemma.

Proof. Suppose all private attachments at leaves lie at at most one
leaf, called q. Choose q arbitrarily if no leaf is attached.

If a0a1 is present, take
  Q={z,p,q}, M={a0a1,zr,ps}.
These are three disjoint edges. Outside Q, the only possible edge is
a0a1, which belongs to M. Thus the link is WKE.

If a0a1 is absent and qa_i is present for some i, take
  Q={z,p,q}, M={qa_i,pr,zs}.
Now Q covers the entire link, and M has three edges, so again it is WKE.

If there is neither a private edge nor any attached leaf, take
  Q={z,p}, M={pr,zs}.
Again Q covers the entire link, and |Q|=|M|. All cases contradict
non-WKE, proving the lemma.

3. TWO NECESSARY CONDITIONS FOR A PATH-PLUS-ISOLATED LINK

Lemma P. Suppose H has precisely the edges pq,pr, with s isolated.
If the link is connected and not WKE, then:
(i) at least one of q,r has a private neighbor;
(ii) if just one of q,r has private neighbors, p is adjacent to both
     private vertices.

Proof of (i). Suppose neither q nor r has a private neighbor.

If a0a1 is present, take
  Q={z,p,s}, M={a0a1,pq,zr}.
Outside Q the only possible edge is a0a1, which belongs to M.

If a0a1 is absent but sa_i is present for some i, take
  Q={z,p,s}, M={sa_i,pq,zr}.
Here Q covers the entire link. Finally, if there is no private edge
and no attachment at s, all attachments are at p; take
  Q={z,p}, M={pq,zs}.
Here Q again covers the entire link. In each case M is a matching
and |Q|=|M|, a contradiction.

Proof of (ii). Suppose r has no private neighbor, and p is not
adjacent to both private vertices. Label a1 so that pa1 is absent.
Put Q={z,q,a0}. The vertices outside Q are p,r,s,a1. Their only
possible edges are pr and sa1.

If sa1 is present, M={pr,sa1,zq} is a matching containing every edge
outside Q. If sa1 is absent, connectivity forces a1q or a1a0 to
be present: all other possible neighbors of a1 have been excluded.
In the former case take M={pr,a1q,zs}; in the latter take
M={pr,a1a0,zs}. In both cases the only edge outside Q is pr,
which belongs to M. Thus |Q|=|M|=3 gives WKE, a contradiction.
This proves (ii).

4. UNIVERSAL OPPOSITE-LEAF REDUCIBILITY CONSTRUCTION

Lemma R. Suppose the core has edges pq,pr and possibly ps, and no
other edges. Suppose qa and rb exist, where a belongs to A and b
belongs to B. Then {u,v} is reducible, with five packed triangles.
No assumption on other attachments, private edges, WKE, or the
degree budget is needed for this sufficient lemma.

Proof. Define

  S={uvs, upr, uqa, vpq, vrb},
  X={uv,ua0,ua1,vb0,vb1,pq,pr,qa,rb}
      union ({ps} if ps is present, otherwise the empty set).

All triangles exist. The two u-only triangles use disjoint rim pairs
{p,r} and {q,a}; the two v-only triangles use disjoint rim pairs
{p,q} and {r,b}. Neither uses s, so the central triangle uvs
shares no spoke with them. Their four rim edges are distinct.
Consequently S is edge-disjoint and |S|=5. Also |X| is nine if
ps is absent and ten if ps is present, so |X| <= 2|S|.

Every triangle containing both hubs meets uv. A triangle containing
u and a private A-vertex meets an A-spoke in X; otherwise its rim
edge is a core edge and belongs to X. The symmetric statement holds
at v. Thus X hits all hub triangles. The only packed edges avoiding
both hubs are pr,qa,pq,rb, and all four are in X.

These statements hold in the ambient graph: every triangle through
a hub uses vertices in its neighborhood. An A-B edge cannot occur
in such a triangle; edges leaving the local neighborhood cannot occur
either. Hence arbitrary such edges do not affect the witness.
This proves every required reducibility condition.

5. COMPLETE STRUCTURAL CONSEQUENCES

Theorem S. If H=K1,3 and both links are non-WKE, {u,v} is reducible.
Neither link connectivity nor the common-vertex degree budget is needed.

Proof. Lemma S gives at least two leaves with A-neighbors and at least
two leaves with B-neighbors. Choose distinct leaves q and r from these
two sets, respectively, and label the remaining leaf s. Apply Lemma R.

Theorem P. If H=P3 plus an isolated vertex, both links are connected
and non-WKE, and the common-vertex degree budget holds at the center
p of P3, then {u,v} is reducible.

Proof. By Lemma P(i), each link's private attachments meet at least
one of the two actual path leaves q,r. If one can choose distinct
leaves with an A-neighbor and a B-neighbor, apply Lemma R, with s
the isolated core vertex.

Otherwise the two nonempty leaf-support sets are the same singleton.
By Lemma P(ii), p has two A-neighbors and two B-neighbors. Since
deg_H(p)=2, its budget sum is 2+2+2=6, contradicting the upper
bound five. This proves the theorem.

6. SCOPE

These are complete human proofs for core types P3+isolated and K1,3
(masks 3 and 7 in the existing bit convention). The saved catalogue
has 150 and 113 records respectively; these counts play no role in
the proof. Lemma R is a specialization of template O2, but its full
packing, cover, and ambient safety have been proved above directly.

Theorems P and S do not settle the P4 core, the C4 core, or the
remaining core types. They can be combined with the separate 2K2
proof in compression/theory/human_case_2k2.txt without asserting a
structural proof for every saved record.
