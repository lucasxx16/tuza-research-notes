P3+K2 endpoint repair review (C; candidate sketch)

ACCEPT the explicit sufficient lemma. Let adjacent u,v have d(u)+d(v)≤15 and common W={a,b,c,p,q}=K5−{ab,bc}; let w outside {u,v}∪W neighbor u with wa,wc present. The packing S={uva,apq,ubp,vbq,ucq,vcp,wac} has seven existing edge-disjoint triangles. The first six are a Fano decomposition after removing the line abc, whose missing edges are exactly ab,bc and whose third edge ac becomes free. The new triangle wac uses ac,wa,wc and no earlier edge. X=E(W)∪{wa,wc,uv}∪all u/v spokes to neighbors outside {opposite hub}∪W has 8+3+(d(u)−6)+(d(v)−6)=d(u)+d(v)−1≤14 edges. Any ambient hub triangle either uses uv, has a rim vertex outside W and meets a deleted hub spoke, or has both rim vertices in W and meets E(W). Packed edges outside {u,v} are core edges plus wa,wc, all in X.

For complement P3+K2 with missing ab,bc,de, choosing v=d gives common W={a,b,c,f,g} and u-private w=e adjacent to all W, hence the lemma applies whenever d(v)≤8. The same holds for v=e. This repairs the round-four case-split gap but does not alone classify every other three-edge complement or eliminate degree-five/six vertices. A named-graph MILP witness would corroborate one instance only; the human lemma is ambient and universal.
