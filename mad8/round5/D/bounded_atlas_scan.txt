ACTUAL CHECKED RANGE.
- 906 connected triangle-bearing atlas graphs of orders 3 through 7.
- 27 of these satisfy tau(H)=2nu(H).
- Zero triangle-containing edges e among those tight graphs satisfy
  tau(H-e)=tau(H).
- Zero timeouts in the checked range.

The first stage therefore found no necessary edge for the richer
three-edge separator profile, and the induced-star/path profile stage
was not triggered. This is not a report of a separately executed
three-edge-profile enumeration.

EXACT MEANING OF THE NEGATIVE RESULT.
For each of the 27 tight graphs, EVERY edge that belongs to a triangle
belongs to SOME minimum triangle cover. The minimum cover may depend
on the edge. The result does not say that one minimum cover contains
all triangle edges, or that every minimum cover contains every edge.

Lemma 1 in equality_boundary_profiles.txt proves the equivalence between
an edge lying in no minimum cover and tau(H-e)=tau(H). Thus none of
the connected graphs in this checked range has the necessary edge
property of the rich separator obstruction. No conclusion is asserted
for graphs of order eight or larger.
