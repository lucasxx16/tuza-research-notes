AN ALTERNATE EIGHT-TRIANGLE CERTIFICATE FOR A K4-MINUS-EDGE CORE

This symbolic construction was obtained before the bounded search found
the smaller seven-triangle certificate used in the main proof file
p3_plus_edge_complements.txt. The hypotheses differ: this construction
does not require the u-private edge ab, but uses more private-core
attachments. No comparison of global optimality is claimed.

Lemma. Let u,v be adjacent degree-seven vertices with common
neighborhood C={x,y,p,q}, inducing K4 minus xy. Let their private
pairs be A={a,b} at u and B={c,d} at v. Suppose

  ax, by, cd, dx, dy, dp, dq

are present. Then {u,v} is reducible. The edge ab need not be
present, and no other private-core attachment is required.
Edges between the two private pairs are unrestricted.

Proof. Set

  S={uax,uby,upq,vxp,vyq,vcd,dxq,dyp},
  R=E(G[C]) union {dx,dy,dp,dq,ax,by,cd},
  X=R union {uv,ua,ub,vc}.

The three u-triangles use disjoint rim pairs {a,x}, {b,y}, {p,q}.
The three v-triangles use disjoint pairs {x,p}, {y,q}, {c,d}.
The two triangles avoiding the hubs use core edges xq,yp and
the four distinct d-core edges dx,dq,dy,dp. The core edges used
are pq,xp,yq,xq,yp, each exactly once. The remaining rim edges
ax,by,cd are distinct. Thus S is edge-disjoint and |S|=8.

The set R has 5+4+3=12 edges. The four additional hub edges
are distinct from each other and from R, so |X|=16=2|S|.

Every triangle through both hubs meets uv. A triangle through u
and a private vertex meets ua or ub; otherwise its rim lies in
E(G[C]). A triangle through v and c meets vc. A triangle through
v and d without c or u meets a d-core edge in R. The triangle
vcd meets cd. All other v-triangles meet a core edge. These
cases exhaust the ambient hub triangles.

The external packing edges are exactly R, proving the safety
condition. Edges between A and B cannot create another hub
triangle, and edges leaving the displayed local vertex set do
not affect the specified complete hub neighborhoods.
