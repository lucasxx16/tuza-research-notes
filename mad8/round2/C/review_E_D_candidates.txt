Round 2 C: adversarial review of E and D candidate lemmas

E's third K5-minus-one-edge repair is sound as relayed. In a Fano
decomposition of K7, choose the line uxy containing the missing core
edge xy. Removing this line removes the sole use of xy and frees ux;
the triangle uwx restores a seven-triangle packing when w is u's
exclusive neighbor and wx exists. The cover has nine existing core
edges, uv, uw, wx, and at most two v-exclusive spokes. It meets every
ambient hub triangle and contains each external packing edge, with
size at most fourteen.

For a degree-seven u whose link complement is a matching of size
0..3, E's matched/unmatched neighbor split is exhaustive. A neighbor
v of degree at most eight yields either a common K5, an induced C4
plus another common vertex, or the repaired K5-minus-edge case.
Thus absence of such a reducible pair makes all neighbors of these
degree-seven vertices have degree at least nine. Conditional on
minimum degree seven and every degree-seven vertex having this link
type, a donor adjacent to L has at least five non-L neighbors.
Transfers of 1/4 give L charge 35/4 and degree-9-or-more donor charge
at least eight, forcing average degree at least
8+3|L|/(4|V|). This is not a full mad<8 proof.

D's initial three-separator gluing proof is sound. For a boundary of
at most two edges, one packing gain pays for deleting that boundary.
For a shared triangle, tightness forces each boundary edge to be
individually gainful somewhere; different gainful edges cannot occur
on opposite sides. A cover of the gainful side contains a boundary
edge e; combining it with a cover of the other side minus e stays
within 2nu(G). Consequently a smallest counterexample in a
subgraph-closed class is 4-connected, after excluding order at most
four directly. This does not establish that a counterexample exists.
