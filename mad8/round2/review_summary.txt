ROUND 2 COORDINATOR DECISION

Target: mad(G)<8 implies tau(G)<=2nu(G).
Decision: substantial conditional progress; full question unresolved.

Accepted mathematical results:
- E/matching_complement_links.txt gives three universal S/X recipes:
  common K5, common induced C4 plus a fifth common vertex, and a repaired
  Fano construction on a common K5 minus an edge with an extra attachment.
  Each verifies ambient hub-triangle coverage and outside-pair packing-edge
  containment. The degree-sum budget is at most fifteen.
- Their exhaustive symbolic matching case split proves that a degree-seven
  vertex with link complement a matching forms a reducible pair with every
  neighbor of degree at most eight.
- The conditional density bound is 8+3|L|/(4n), for minimum degree at least
  seven, matching complements at degree-seven links, and all neighbors of
  those vertices of degree at least nine. No sharpness claim is made.
- D/three_edge_boundary.txt proves gluing across separators of order at
  most three under Tuza for all boundary-edge-deleted sides. A smallest
  counterexample in any subgraph-closed class must be 4-connected.
- D also gives necessary packing-gain profiles, cover avoidance/tightness,
  and a P4/K1,3 restriction for four-vertex cuts with at most three edges.

Scope:
These are conditional forcing and gluing theorems. The link condition is
not hereditary under arbitrary edge deletion, so no induction on that
subclass is asserted. Vertices of degree five/six and degree-seven links
whose complement has maximum degree at least two remain unresolved.
Neither new witnesses nor minimal-counterexample conditions imply that
a counterexample exists. No novelty or formal-verification claim is made.

Computation and rejected outputs:
A single bounded optimizer attempt for one named graph used a mistaken
hub/core label in the outside-packing-edge constraint. Its reported
eight-triangle/nine-cover output was rejected, not interpreted as a proof.
The hand five-triangle/ten-cover certificate was checked using corrected
code and an independent implementation. A separate repaired-Fano checker
initially enumerated edge tuples instead of vertex triples and checked
packing membership instead of coverage by X; that implementation was
rejected and repaired. The human proofs never used either faulty output.
Unused search artifacts are preserved privately, not published as results.
No upstream census or original certificate finder was rerun.

The coordinator reran the corrected literal checks. A targeted negative
control also passed: replacing cover edge (2,4) by the real spoke (0,4)
preserves the repaired-Fano cover size and X subset E, but the checker
rejects it with nonzero exit for uncovered hub triangles and a missing
external packing edge. This directly exercises the formerly faulty checks.

Literature audit:
The final A notes preserve the irreducible/robust hypotheses, distinguish
degree at most six from degree exactly six, and identify Botler Lemma 3.2
as a restatement of Puleo's result. Transfer of the local necessary
conditions to a minimal mad<8 counterexample was already justified;
this is not a transfer of the entire mad<7 theorem to mad<8.

Next research target: the smallest degree-seven link-complement patterns
