TUTTE--BERGE: VERIFIED CLASSICAL INPUT

Alexander Schrijver, "The Tutte-Berge formula", author-hosted CWI note,
page 1, displayed theorem (1):
https://homepages.cwi.nl/~lex/files/tutteb.pdf

Michel X. Goemans, "Lecture notes on non-bipartite matching",
MIT 18.433, 13 February 2011, page 1, Theorem 2.1:
https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/18433S11/matching-nonbip-notes.pdf

Both state mu(F)=min_S (|V(F)|+|S|-o(F-S))/2, where o counts odd
components. Since the sum of floor(|K|/2) over components K of F-S
equals (|V(F)|-|S|-o(F-S))/2, this is the component-sum form used
in the independent proof. The coordinator opened and checked both
public sources. The classical formula is an imported theorem.
