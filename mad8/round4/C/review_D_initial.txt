Preliminary D seven-edge-cut audit (C)

No counterexample yet to D/independent_initial.txt. For an ordinary-matching-number-two graph, the Tutte--Berge equality cases |S|=2,1,0 reduce respectively to an independent remainder; one 3-vertex or 2-vertex nontrivial remainder plus isolates; or components of size at most five. The asserted WKE classification is plausible but requires the full witness proof, especially when the distinguished S-vertex also meets isolated vertices. A size-four matching in the bipartite cut graph gives four pairwise disjoint cut edges, hence an independent four-set in the compatibility graph. If that compatibility graph has matching number three, its complement vertex set is a cover of size at most three and yields WKE. If matching number at most two and non-WKE, the proposed five-edge K5-minus-matching block would need five pairwise intersecting cut edges, a star; two remaining cut edges cannot raise the cut-graph matching number from one to four.

For a cut component containing C4 under seven total edges and cut-graph matching number at least four, a C4 plus at most two attachments is forced. The possible matching-three six-edge components are two pendants on adjacent cycle vertices or a pendant path of length two; opposite/same-shore pendant placements have matching number at most two. The proposed rim-count certificates appear plausible. The full proof must label each crossing-triangle choice, show all three/four-rim cases, and explain why certificates on distinct cut components combine without repeating an internal rim. This is preliminary acceptance of the strategy, not final acceptance of the seven-cut theorem.

Editorial clarification: the preceding shape claim requires a component containing C4, not just any cyclic component. A C6 component is instead handled by the C4-free argument in the final proof.
