ROUND 3 COORDINATOR DECISION

Target: mad(G)<8 implies tau(G)<=2nu(G).
Decision: reviewed conditional and structural progress; full question open.

Accepted mainline result:
E/p3_complement_links.txt extends the local forcing theorem from all
matching-complement degree-seven links to those links together with P3
plus four isolated vertices. Nonexceptional low-degree neighbors use the
previous K5 lemma; mutually exceptional degree-seven endpoints use the
attributed round-one D certificate. No new construction is claimed for
that reused eight-triangle/sixteen-cover recipe.

In the absence of the listed reductions, L is independent and every
degree-seven vertex has six neighbors of degree at least nine. The
separate density lemma requires precisely that independence, the six
high neighbors, and link minimum degree four. It yields average degree
at least 8+|H|/(6n). The unresolved exceptional (7,8) pair is permitted
by this argument, not assumed reducible.

E/charge_tradeoff.txt's uniform-transfer refinement permits h high neighbors and link
minimum degree r whenever h+r>=9, with transfer 1/h, still assuming
minimum degree at least seven and independence of L. In particular five
high neighbors and r=4 suffice to force average degree at least eight.
This is a conditional counting tool, not a new link classification.

Accepted independent result:
D/small_edge_cuts.txt proves that a minimum counterexample in any class
closed under arbitrary subgraphs has minimum degree at least five and
every complete bipartition cut of size at most six isolates one vertex.
It proves its cut-edge reduction and induction bridge, the needed small
WKE classification, the C4 exception, and the bipartite matching-cover
identity. The previous reviewed 4-connectivity theorem is the only
research result imported. Degree-five/six singleton cuts remain allowed.

Computation scope:
One named 11-vertex/44-edge graph was tested under two extra certificate
restrictions: packed triangles avoid the three v-exclusive vertices,
and all three corresponding v-spokes belong to X. A first solver run
used equality in the budget row; C changed it to the intended <= bound,
and B reran the same graph. Both statuses are only numerical diagnostics.
Equality was feasibility-equivalent here by padding X with graph edges,
but made the slack objective vacuous. No exact infeasibility certificate
or unrestricted nonreducibility claim follows. The main proofs use none
of these solver outputs. No upstream census or finder was rerun.

Literature audit:

They already apply in a subgraph-minimal mad<8 counterexample; whether
