Exact-file review of D/small_edge_cuts.txt (C)

ACCEPT. The definition of edge-set reduction is sufficient: G'=G−(E0∪X) is proper, packed S is disjoint from every packing in G', and X plus a cover of G' covers G. For a cut δ(U), crossing triangles correspond one-to-one to edges of the compatibility graph H. A WKE matching selects triangles without repeated cut edges; when the cut graph B is C4-free, selected rims cannot repeat. Q plus those rims is a valid X with |X|≤2|S|. The separate B=C4 certificate covers both potential internal rims and permits isolated-edge components.

The five-vertex WKE classification is correct: a complement vertex of degree≥2 yields a witness by the three cases in the file; otherwise the complement is a matching and every remaining triple spans at least two incident edges, preventing WKE. The bipartite matching-cover proof is standard and complete. In a 4-connected, δ≥5 minimal counterexample, a nontrivial ≤6 cut has shores of size≥4 and cut graph matching number≥4, so total deficiency≤2. This excludes components of six or more edges; a five-edge component must be a six-vertex tree with perfect matching and cannot have non-WKE K5-minus-matching compatibility. Cyclic components reduce to the direct C4 case. Thus every nontrivial ≤6 cut is reducible, contradicting minimality. The conclusion is conditional on a smallest counterexample in an arbitrary-subgraph-closed class; it does not imply Tuza for mad<8.
