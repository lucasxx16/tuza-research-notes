Preliminary D small-cut audit (C)

D/independent_initial.txt appears mathematically sound, pending a full edge-set-reduction induction statement. A crossing triangle uses exactly two cut edges and one internal rim. For C4-free bipartite cut graph, two distinct crossing triangles cannot share a rim: otherwise their four cut edges form a C4. A matching in the compatibility graph therefore selects edge-disjoint crossing triangles, and WKE supplies cut edges Q covering every unselected crossing triangle with |Q|<=|M|. Taking X as Q plus selected rims gives |X|<=2|S| and protects every packed edge outside the cut.

The C4 cut graph with four edges is a separate direct certificate: at most two shore-internal diagonals support all crossing triangles; put those present diagonals in X and select one supported triangle if any exists. For a nontrivial cut of at most six edges in a 4-connected, δ>=5 graph, each shore has at least four vertices; a cut-graph vertex cover of size<=3 would disconnect the surviving shores, so König gives ν(B)>=4 and m−ν(B)<=2. A component with at least six edges would alone contribute deficiency>=3. A five-edge component must be a six-vertex tree with perfect matching; its cut-edge compatibility graph cannot be K5 minus a matching because the tree contains three pairwise disjoint cut edges. This supports the WKE decomposition.

The final proof should explicitly define reducibility for an EDGE set and show that deleting E0∪X gives a proper subgraph to which minimality applies. Otherwise the local certificate has no formal bridge to the claimed minimal-counterexample structure. This is a request for exposition, not a found counterexample.
