Round 1 C adversarial review of initial mad<8 candidates

E's K8 pair lemma is sound as relayed. A Fano seven-triangle packing on
u,v and five clique vertices uses each edge of that K7 once. The ten
edges of the five-vertex rim, uv, and two spokes to the sixth clique
vertex cover all triangles through u or v and contain every packing
edge avoiding the hubs. This is 13 edges; one external hub neighbor
needs at most one more spoke, within 2|S|=14. Therefore, in an
irreducible graph, every neighbor of a simplicial degree-seven vertex
has degree at least nine.

E's conditional discharging is also correct: under delta>=7 and all
degree-seven vertices simplicial, the set L of degree-seven vertices
is independent. Each neighbor of L has six other neighbors outside L
in a K7 link, so d_L(v)<=d(v)-6. Giving 1/5 per incident L edge leaves
each L vertex with 42/5 and each degree-at-least-nine donor with at
least (4d+6)/5>=42/5; degree-eight vertices stay at eight. Thus the
average is at least eight, a contradiction to mad<8. This identifies
unsettled low-degree or nonsimplicial-seven branches, not a full proof.

D's maximal degree-seven-pair codegree-four patch has ten vertices,
37 edges, minimum degree six, and mad=37/5. Its connected links have
four universal core vertices and are non-WKE, while core vertices
have degree nine. All fixed-hub local triples are subgraphs of it.
Hence mad<8 alone cannot restore the old common-vertex budget <=5.
This is a failure of a local proof inference, not a Tuza counterexample.
