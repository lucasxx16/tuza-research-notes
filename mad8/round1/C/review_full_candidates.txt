I accept E/mixed_fano_and_density.txt and E/obligations.txt under their
stated hypotheses. E's seven Fano triples use the 21 edges of a K7
once. Its 13-edge K8 cover meets every hub triangle and contains all
packing edges outside the pair; one external hub neighbor costs one
additional spoke. The conditional 1/5 discharging counts six non-L

eight at degree eight. K7 joined to three independent vertices attains
the 42/5 bound. K9 minus one edge has mad=70/9, connected non-WKE
links, and no 7--7 edge, yet E's mixed (7,8) pair reduces it.
