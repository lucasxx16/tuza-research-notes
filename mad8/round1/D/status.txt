MAD<8 / D / ROUND 1 / REVIEW MILESTONE

All three full arguments are reviewed research proofs; see
C/review_full_candidates.txt. This records internal research review,
not journal peer review or formal machine verification.

1. local_density_obstruction.txt:
   Every reduced patch with two degree-seven hubs at codegree four has
   mad<=37/5; the maximal patch attains this and has connected non-WKE
   hub links, but core degree nine. Thus mad<8 alone excludes no old
   codegree-four local triple and cannot recover the degree-seven core
   budget. Analogous sharp patch bounds: 22/3 at codegree five, seven
   at codegree six. At codegrees at most five these patches have an
   explicit width-six tree decomposition, so ordinary Tuza already
   holds; the relative external-edge safety condition is the bottleneck.

2. dense_pair_certificate.txt:
   A budget-free positive extension: for K4 core, both private edges
   present, and one private A-vertex complete to the core, the designated
   hubs have an explicit eight-triangle/sixteen-edge certificate, with
   every other attachment arbitrary. This applies to the maximal patch
   in (1), demonstrating that the density obstruction is not itself an
   obstruction to reducibility. Proof uses no non-WKE assumptions.

3. two_separator_gluing.txt:
   Exact packing formula for gluing two sides along an edge; Tuza on the
   sides and their edge-deleted versions implies Tuza on the union.
   There is one unit of slack if the shared edge is unavoidable in a
   maximum packing on either side. Thus a smallest mad<8 counterexample
   must be 3-connected. No novelty claim is made for this elementary lemma.
