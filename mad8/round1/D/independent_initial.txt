Independent D draft for the mad(G)<8 question.

Chosen direction: a local-density obstruction to extending the degree-seven
pair proof, with possible follow-up through boundary versions of known
triangle-packing theorems. This was written before consulting any other
new mad8 route. Read only the source question and reducibility definitions,
and Puleo's primary-source abstract.

Candidate lemma. Every codegree-four local configuration with hubs of
degree seven, four common neighbors, two private vertices on each side,
and NO edges between the private sides is itself a graph of maximum
average degree at most 37/5=7.4. No link hypothesis or pointwise common-
neighbor degree budget is needed.

Reason. The maximal such graph J has every allowed edge. It has 10
vertices and 37 edges: 13 hub edges, six core edges, and nine side
edges on each side. Its minimum degree is six. An induced subgraph on
nine vertices therefore has at most 31 edges, giving average at most
62/9<7.4. A subgraph on at most eight vertices has average at most seven.
Thus mad(J)=74/10=37/5; every local configuration is a subgraph of J.

The maximal graph J has both hub degrees seven, connected non-WKE links,
and core degrees nine. For the non-WKE assertion, each link has four
universal core vertices on seven total vertices. A WKE witness has a
vertex set Q of size at most three, so leaves a universal core vertex
outside Q and at least four total vertices outside Q. The induced graph
outside Q has degree at least three there, and cannot be a matching.
Thus the pointwise budget deg_H(c)+deg_A(c)+deg_B(c)<=5 from the
maximum-degree-seven argument fails here: its left side is seven.

Consequently every arbitrary local triple, including every connected
non-WKE triple discarded solely by the degree budget, is realizable
inside the proposed mad<8 class by its own local graph. An average-degree
hypothesis strictly greater than 37/5 cannot prune ANY such local triple
without further minimal-counterexample constraints. Recovering the old
pointwise budget directly from mad<8 is impossible.

The analogous maximal codegree-five local graph has nine vertices and
33 edges, hence mad=22/3 (all proper vertex subsets have at most eight
vertices and average at most seven). At codegree six the maximal local
graph is K8, with mad=7. Thus all unconstrained degree-seven pair patches
at codegrees four, five, and six already lie below 7.4.

This is an obstruction to one proposed proof mechanism, not a counterexample
to Tuza. The full mad<8 question remains open in this draft. Next: prove
the lemma formally and identify precisely what boundary information a
pair-reduction argument would need beyond ordinary Tuza on these patches.
