MAD<8 / D / ROUND 1
A DEGREE-BUDGET-FREE CERTIFICATE FOR THE MAXIMAL CODEGREE-FOUR PATCH

Status: Reviewed research proof; see C/review_full_candidates.txt.

PROPOSITION.
Let u,v be adjacent vertices with common neighborhood
C={c0,c1,c2,c3} and exclusive neighborhoods A={a0,a1}, B={b0,b1}.
Suppose C induces K4, both a0a1 and b0b1 are edges, and a1 is adjacent
to all four core vertices. Then {u,v} is reducible, using eight packed
triangles and sixteen cover edges. No upper bound on core degrees, no
non-WKE hypothesis, and no constraint on other A--C or B--C edges is
required. Arbitrary A--B edges are harmless.

Proof. Take the packing

 S={ u c0 c1, u c2 c3, u a0 a1,
     a1 c0 c3, a1 c1 c2,
     v c0 c2, v c1 c3, v b0 b1 }.

Take the twelve-edge rim set

 R=E(G[C]) union {a0a1,b0b1,a1c0,a1c1,a1c2,a1c3},

and the sixteen-edge cover

 X=R union {uv,ua0,vb0,vb1}.

Every triangle exists by the hypotheses. The three u-triangles use the
disjoint spoke pairs {c0,c1}, {c2,c3}, {a0,a1}; the three v-triangles use
the disjoint pairs {c0,c2}, {c1,c3}, {b0,b1}. The two triangles avoiding
the hubs use distinct a1--C spokes and disjoint core edges c0c3,c1c2.
The six core edges used across all triangles are precisely the six
different edges of K4. The private edges are used once each. No other
edge is shared, so S is an eight-triangle packing.

The external packing edges are exactly R: six core edges, four a1--C
edges, and the two private edges. Thus R subset X verifies the safety
condition, and |X|=12+4=16=2|S|.

A triangle through both hubs is met by uv. A triangle through u and
two core vertices is met by its core edge in R; one through u and a1
and a core vertex is met by its a1--C edge in R; one through u and a0
is met by ua0. The triangle u a0 a1 is met by a0a1 (and ua0).
At v, core-only rim triangles meet E(G[C]) subset R, and every triangle
using a B-vertex is met by vb0 or vb1. This includes a possible private
triangle v b0 b1. These are all hub-triangle types.

Finally any triangle containing a hub uses only that hub's neighborhood.
A--B edges and edges leaving the displayed local patch cannot generate
additional such triangles. Thus the certificate is valid in any ambient
graph satisfying the stated neighborhood description.

APPLICATION TO THE DENSITY OBSTRUCTION.
The maximal patch J_4 in local_density_obstruction.txt satisfies these
hypotheses. Hence its designated hubs themselves form a reducible pair,
even though its core vertices have degree nine and violate the earlier
pointwise budget by two. The dense patch is therefore an obstruction
only to recovering that budget from mad<8, not to pair reducibility.

The sufficient condition is broader than the single maximal graph: it
allows all 2^12 choices of the four a0--C and eight B--C attachments.
This count is just the number of freely optional edges; no enumeration
was run. No overall classification or solution of mad<8 is claimed.
