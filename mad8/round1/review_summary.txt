Question: does mad(G)<8 imply tau(G)<=2nu(G)?
Decision: stop at a rigorous partial milestone; full question unresolved.

Accepted after independent adversarial review and coordinator inspection:
- E/mixed_fano_and_density.txt: mixed-degree Fano pair reduction, sharp
  conditional density bound, and K9-e obstruction to a 7--7-only route.
- E/obligations.txt: remaining degree-five/six and nonsimplicial-seven cases.

  with no bound on common-neighbor degrees.
- D/two_separator_gluing.txt: exact gluing identity and 3-connectivity

The full arguments are self-contained except explicitly cited background
results. The treewidth consequence uses Botler--Fernandes--Gutierrez,
arXiv:2002.07925v2, Theorem 3.4, checked against the primary text.
The Fano base construction is attributed to the source manuscript.
There is no claim of novelty, formal verification, or human peer review.

Important scope decisions:
- A forced reducible pair under non-hereditary extra assumptions is not
  automatically a Tuza theorem for the entire class with those assumptions.
- The patch-density examples refute a proposed budget inference, not Tuza.
- Connected non-WKE links are necessary local constraints, not a synonym
  for absence of every reducible vertex set.
- Ordinary Tuza on a patch does not supply the external-packing-edge
  containment condition needed for a relative pair reduction.
- The gluing generalization requires closure under arbitrary subgraphs;
  closure only under induced subgraphs would not justify deleting an edge.

Rejected literature-draft inferences (not used in accepted proofs):
- mad<8 could contain an 8-regular subgraph or K9: false by definition.
- Subgraph minimality would be unavailable: false; mad<8 is monotone.
- Excluding long odd wheels would make every link bipartite: false;
  triangles in links are still possible under the cited corollary.
- Wang's fixed-system optimal weights rule out every improved mad-specific
  use of that system: unsupported; extra parameter constraints may help.
See C/review_A_literature.txt for source corrections and the final A notes.
