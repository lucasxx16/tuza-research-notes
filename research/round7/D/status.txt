Theorem A gives an ambient-safe six-triangle reduction for independent
centers with degree sum at most eleven, sharing a chosen K4, when a
neighbor r of the second center is complete to that K4.

Theorem B adds one triangle and permits degree sum at most twelve
when a further neighbor q of the second center is adjacent to r.
The cover budget explicitly absorbs one additional incident edge.

Together with Puleo Lemma 2.7(a),(b), this excludes a common K4 between
degree-five and degree-six vertices in any robust graph with no
reducible set. For a degree-six/six pair sharing K4, each six-link's
sole missing edge must join its two vertices outside that chosen K4.

The round-six twelve-vertex graph J has a reduction on {b,t} using
six triangles and twelve cover edges. This is a local certificate in
an arbitrary ambient graph, not just a verification of Tuza on J.
