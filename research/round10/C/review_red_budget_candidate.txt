ROUND 10 C — PRIVATE-RED RED-EDGE-BUDGET CANDIDATE

E's proposed hypothesis is exact: H has every triangle one-red, Q is a maximum packing of p triangles, each Q red edge is private among all H triangles, and r participating red edges obeys r≤3p. No general inequality tau(H)≤(2−1/100)p has been proved or refuted here. The unbudgeted blue-apex/triangle-free-base example does not refute it: D's exact matching argument gives beta(R)≤2p−p²/(2r−p)≤9p/5 under r≤3p. A one-triangle sanity check is stronger: when p=1, the private red edge forces all H triangles to share one blue edge of Q, so tau=1. For the Wang application, H=G'−B(P') has no type-0, type-1, or type-3 triangle by maximality of P,A, and lexicographic P' respectively; thus every H triangle is one-red. Its participating red edges lie in R(B*), so r≤b, and at the algebraic face b=3c. These facts make the candidate relevant but do not establish a uniform bonus or a global coefficient improvement.

E's subsequent local exchange is valid: each Q triangle has at most one blue edge supporting a one-contact triangle. Two such pages on opposite blue edges would either augment Q, share a third vertex and create a second page on Q's private red spine, or create an all-red triangle. Double-contact triangles remain uncontrolled. E's conditional transfer is also valid: delete all three edges of c−h nonprivate Q pages, then delete enough other participating red edges to bring the remaining red count to 3h, apply the hypothesized bonus, and restore the deleted edges. This yields tau(H)≤3(c−h)+(2−epsilon)h+max(0,b−c−2h). At the retained-W5 equality point h=c,b=3c it is strictly stronger; the original four-row h=0 equality face is excluded by W5. Compactness would then give a strictly smaller universal coefficient, but ONLY if the colored red-budget lemma is proved.

The stronger ALL-participating-red-private subclass is solved symbolically. Form J with one vertex per blue edge of H and one J edge per H triangle joining its two blue edges. Global red privacy gives ν(H)=matching(J) and τ(H)=vertex-cover(J), because each red cover edge can be replaced by a blue edge of its sole triangle. Every J triangle would correspond to three pairwise intersecting blue edges: either a blue triangle, forbidden outright, or a blue star whose three leaf-pair edges are red, yielding a forbidden red triangle. Hence J is triangle-free, |E(J)|=r, and D's estimate yields τ(H)≤2p−p²/(2r−p), at most 9p/5 for r≤3p. This does not address red edges outside Q that have multiple pages.

For general H let s be the number of participating red edges that are globally nonprivate. Deleting them leaves the all-private subclass, preserves the h private Q pages, and gives a maximum remaining packing k∈[h,c] with t=r−s≥k red edges. For h>0, monotonicity of k²/(2t−k) and t≤b−s yields τ(H)≤2c+s−h²/[2(b−s)−h]; the denominator is positive. At b=3c,h=c, equality τ=2c requires s/c≥(5−sqrt(17))/4. This is a necessary structural condition, not a uniform improvement when s is large.

The explicit conditional amplification also checks. Write n=1, c0=12/59, Δ=165/59−τ, e=1−a−m, l=c−h, and g_i=W_i−τ≥0. Wang's weighted sum and W5 imply 93e+d+3(l−6d)_+≤59Δ, g3≤4Δ, g4≤5Δ. Consequently l≤354Δ, c≥c0−65Δ, h≥c0−419Δ, and b−3c≤243Δ. The hypothetical colored bonus bridge then yields εh≤1222Δ, hence Δ≥12ε/(59·1641)>ε/10000 for 0<ε≤1. This conclusion remains conditional on a proof of the central r≤3p lemma.
