The saved red_budget/results.json and report.txt are invalid as triangle edge-cover data. In red_budget_search.py, min_cover(n,tv) enumerates subsets of range(n), where n is the number of VERTICES, and tv consists of triangle vertex triples. It computes a vertex set hitting every triangle, not a set of GRAPH EDGES hitting every triangle. This exactly explains reported tau/p=1/2 and 1/1 despite nonempty edge-disjoint packings. dump() repeats the same vertex-based cover and minimality checks.
