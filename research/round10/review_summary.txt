4. E extends that estimate through an auxiliary graph to the class where
EVERY participating red edge is private. It proves tau(H)<=9p/5 when
r<=3p. This is stronger privacy than the unresolved candidate requires.
Deleting s nonprivate participating red edges gives the more general
bound
  tau(H)<=2c+s-h^2/(2(b-s)-h), h>0.
The h=0 boundary is handled separately. At the abstract critical profile,
this bound can leave tau(H)=2c possible only if
  s/c >= (5-sqrt(17))/4.
This necessary condition neither constructs nor excludes such a residual.

5. The candidate RB(epsilon) is explicitly UNPROVED. It assumes every
triangle is one-red, existence of an all-private maximum packing, and
r<=3p. E's deletion bridge and exact conditional implication are valid:

for 0<epsilon<=1 and every finite simple G. The rational constants and
the zero-packing boundary were checked. RB(1/100) would give a gain of
1/1000000, but no such unconditional gain is claimed.

The proofs use complete triangle families and maximum packings; replacing
either by a selected subfamily or a merely maximal packing is invalid.
No graph-order bound, mad bound, enumeration, or numerical output is a
