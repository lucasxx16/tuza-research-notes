ROUND 10 / D
RETAINED SLACK AND THE LIMITS OF A PRIVATE-RED IMPROVEMENT

Status: reviewed research proof; see C/review_D_full.txt.
No universal constant below 165/59 is proved in this file. No claim
of literature novelty is made. The work gives a quantitative version
of retained slack and settles a precise proposed colored-family
improvement negatively, with an explicit red-budget scope boundary.
No mad<8 hypothesis or numerical search is used.

1. NOTATION AND THE PRECISE MISSING GAIN.

For a family F of triangles, nu(F) is the largest number of pairwise
edge-disjoint members and tau(F) the smallest number of graph edges
meeting every member. In a red-blue graph H, F(H) consists of ALL
triangles with exactly one red edge. An edge is private in F if
exactly one member of F contains it.

Use Wang's Section 3 notation:
  n=nu(G), P an original maximum packing, O=E(P);
  A a maximum packing of triangles with one O-edge, a=|A|;
  G'=G-E(A);
  P' a packing maximizing first its type-2 count b, then its size m;
  S the type-2 family after deleting all old edges of P';
  Q a maximum packing of S, c=|Q|;
  h the number of Q-members with red edges private in S;
  Q1 a maximum packing after also deleting the blue edges of Q,
  d=|Q1|.
Here new edges are red, old edges blue, and e=n-a-m>=0.
The new-edge graph is triangle-free: otherwise a triangle avoiding
O could be added to the original maximum packing P.

The following construction estimates are imported from Wang's proof,
retaining its exact a+m terms rather than replacing them by n:
  (W1) tau(G)<=3n-a;
  (W2) tau(G)<=3n/2+5a/2+3b/2-c+3h/8;
  (W3) tau(G)<=3(a+m)-c-d;
  (W4) tau(G)<=3(a+m)-b+9c/4+3d/2-h/4.
Wang's intermediate second colored transversal also gives
  tau(S)<=2c+3d-r<=2c+3d,
where r counts private red edges in the next surviving packing and
is nonnegative. Thus the same outer cover gives the additional bound
  (W5) tau(G)<=3(a+m)-b+2c+3d.
This cover is already present as an intermediate construction in
Wang's Lemma 2.3. It is not claimed as a new construction here.

PROPOSITION 1 (RETAINED SLACK).
With this notation,
  59 tau(G) <= 165n - 93e - d - 3(c-h-6d)_+,
where x_+=max(x,0).

Proof. Weight W1,W2,W3,W4 by 20,8,19,12, respectively. The a,b,c,h
coefficients cancel. The d coefficient is -1. Replacing the two
3n terms of Wang's final displayed estimates by 3(a+m)=3n-3e
retains an additional -(19+12)*3e=-93e. Consequently
  59 tau(G)<=165n-93e-d.
The right-hand side of W4 minus the right-hand side of W5 equals
  (c-h-6d)/4.
Using the better of W4 and W5 in that same weighted sum therefore
subtracts 12*(c-h-6d)_+/4, proving the assertion.

For example, a graph with
  tau(G)>(165/59-epsilon)n
must have
  93e+d+3(c-h-6d)_+<59 epsilon n.
This is a precise necessary small-slack condition near the current
constant. It does not provide a positive uniform lower bound on the
left-hand side, which would be required for a strict universal gain.

2. THE ENHANCED ALGEBRAIC SYSTEM STILL HAS AN EQUALITY POINT.

The nonnegative parameter choice
  a=c=h=12n/59,
  b=36n/59, m=47n/59,
  d=e=0
satisfies a+m=n, d<=c<=b<=m, and h<=c. If n is a multiple
of 59, all listed parameters are integers. At this point every
right-hand side in W1-W5 equals 165n/59. Thus neither retaining
a+m nor adding W5 alone excludes equality in the algebraic system.

This is an algebraic feasibility statement. No graph is claimed to
realize all the packings, coloring, covers, and equalities at once.
The four-inequality optimality already noted in Wang's Remark is
not counted as a result here. The point of this calculation is the
additional all-private endpoint h=c after W5 is retained.

At this endpoint, the colored part has p=c packed triangles, all
their red edges private, and q=d=0 after their blue edges are deleted.
A proposed way to improve would be a fixed delta>0 satisfying
  tau(F)<= (2-delta)p
for every such colored family whose red-edge graph is triangle-free.
The next section disproves this assertion if no bound on the total
number of red edges is imposed. Section 4 explains exactly why this
does not rule out an improvement sensitive to b near b=3c.

3. CONE FAMILIES: AN EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THAT PROPOSED BONUS.

Let R be a nonempty triangle-free graph with matching number p>0,
vertex-cover number beta(R), and independence number alpha(R).
Add a new vertex z adjacent to every vertex of R. Color every edge
of R red and every edge from z blue. Let F be all one-red triangles.
Then
  F={zxy: xy in E(R)}.
Every red edge is private in F. In fact R is triangle-free, so these
are also all triangles of the underlying uncolored graph.

PROPOSITION 2 (EXACT PARAMETERS).
For this family,
  nu(F)=p=mu(R), tau(F)=beta(R)=|V(R)|-alpha(R).
For every maximum packing Q of F, all p red edges are private and
deleting B(Q) leaves no triangle of F.

Proof. Two distinct triangles zxy and zst are edge-disjoint exactly
when xy and st are disjoint edges of R. Thus packings correspond
exactly to matchings, proving the packing identity.
A vertex cover U of R gives the transversal {zx:x in U}, so
tau(F)<=beta(R). Conversely, take any transversal X of F. Every
blue edge in X is zx for some x; put x in a vertex set U. For each
red edge xy in X, put either endpoint in U. This uses at most |X|
vertices. Every edge xy of R is covered by U, because its triangle
zxy was met either by zx, by zy, or by xy. Hence beta(R)<=|X|,
proving equality and the standard complementary independence formula.

A maximum matching leaves an independent set of unmatched vertices:
an edge between two of them could be added to the matching. Deleting
the blue edges of the corresponding packing removes every apex edge
to a matched vertex. A surviving triangle would require an edge of R
between two unmatched vertices, which is impossible. The privacy
assertion holds for every red edge by construction.

PROPOSITION 3 (RATIOS APPROACH TWO).
There are triangle-free graphs R with alpha(R)=o(|V(R)|). Consequently
the cone families above satisfy tau(F)/nu(F)->2 along a sequence,
while every packed red edge is private and the blue-deletion residual
packing number is zero.

Self-contained existence proof. Start with a random graph on N
vertices, choosing each edge independently with probability
  rho=N^(-3/4).
The expected number T of triangles is at most N^(3/4)/6. Markov's
inequality gives
  Pr(T>N^(7/8))<=N^(-1/8)/6 ->0.
Put t=ceil(4N^(3/4) log N). For sufficiently large N, t<N. The expected
number of independent t-sets is at most
  N^t exp(-rho*t*(t-1)/2).
Since rho*(t-1)>=4 log N-rho, its logarithm is at most
  -t*(log N-rho/2),
which tends to minus infinity. By the first-moment bound, with
probability tending to one there is no independent t-set.

For sufficiently large N there is therefore a realization with both
T<=N^(7/8) and alpha<t. Choose one vertex from each of its triangles
and delete the union of the chosen vertices. At most T vertices are
deleted, and the resulting graph R is triangle-free. It has order
  M>=N-N^(7/8)
and independence number below t=o(M). This proves the existence claim.

For these graphs, mu(R)<=M/2 and beta(R)=M-alpha(R), so
  beta(R)/mu(R)>=2-2alpha(R)/M ->2.
The reverse bound beta(R)<=2mu(R) follows by taking all endpoints
of a maximum matching as a vertex cover. Thus the ratios converge
to two. By Proposition 2 the same is true for the colored families.

In particular, for every fixed delta>0, one of these families violates
tau(F)<=(2-delta)nu(F), despite having a triangle-free red graph,
private red edges on every packed triangle, and zero residual q.
This settles only that unbudgeted colored-family strengthening.

4. RED-BUDGET SCOPE: THESE CONES DO NOT OBSTRUCT b APPROXIMATELY 3c.

The enhanced algebraic equality point has b=3c. Here b bounds the
number of red edges available in Wang's residual family. In contrast,
the cones approaching ratio two necessarily have an unbounded ratio
of total red edges to their packing number. The following estimate
makes that distinction quantitative without any numerical search.

LEMMA (TRIANGLE-FREE MATCHING/COVER ESTIMATE).
Let R be triangle-free with p=mu(R)>0 and m=|E(R)|. Then
  beta(R)<=2p-p^2/(2m-p).

Proof. Fix a maximum matching M of size p, and let U be the unmatched
vertex set. It is independent. For each matched edge xy, at least
one endpoint has no neighbor in U. Otherwise take a neighbor s in U
of x and a neighbor t in U of y. If s!=t, replacing xy by xs and yt
increases the matching size. If s=t, then x,y,s form a triangle.
Both are impossible.

Choose one such endpoint from each matched edge, obtaining a set Z
of p vertices with no edges to U. Its induced graph has at most m-p
edges, since none of the p matching edges lies in Z. It has an
independent set I of size at least
  p^2/(2(m-p)+p)=p^2/(2m-p).
To verify this standard elementary bound, order the vertices of
R[Z] uniformly at random and select vertices preceding all their
neighbors. The selected set is independent and has expected size
sum_{x in Z}1/(d_Z(x)+1). Cauchy-Schwarz bounds this sum below by
p^2/(2|E(R[Z])|+p), giving the displayed lower bound.

All matched endpoints form a cover of size 2p. Remove I from it.
The remaining set is still a cover: U is independent, I is
independent, and there are no edges from I to U. Its size is at
most 2p-p^2/(2m-p), proving the lemma.

COROLLARY FOR THE CONE SUBCLASS.
If a cone above has at most Lp red edges, where L>=1, then
  tau(F)<= (2-1/(2L-1))p.
In particular, at a red budget m<=3p it satisfies
  tau(F)<=9p/5.
Conversely, tau(F)/p>=2-epsilon forces
  m/p >= (1+1/epsilon)/2.
Thus a sequence of cones approaching ratio two cannot have a fixed
red-edge/packing budget.

SCOPE DISTINCTION.
The cone counterexamples do NOT refute a strict improvement for
Wang's residual families with b near 3c. The cone-specific 9/5 bound
also does NOT prove such an improvement for all those families:
in a cone every red edge is globally private, whereas h=c in Wang's
notation only makes the red edges of one maximum packing private.
Other red edges may have many pages, and the entire family need not
be representable by matchings of a single base graph.

Therefore an unresolved concrete route is a red-budget-sensitive
colored lemma for that larger class, together with its global
embedding constraints. This file makes no claim about its truth.
