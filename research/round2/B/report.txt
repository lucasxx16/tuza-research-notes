TASK E-TARGETED: Optimized Cover Construction (Bounded)
===============================================================================
For each stored packing S:
  R = union of non-hub (rim) edges of packing triangles [FORCED in any valid cover]
  Ju = rim subgraph induced on C∪A = {2,3,4,5,6,7}
  Jv = rim subgraph induced on C∪B = {2,3,4,5,8,9}

Optimize X from {R, optional uv, hub spokes only}. Vertex covers via 64 subsets.

OPTION 1 (with uv):  |X| = |R| + 1 + vc(Ju-R) + vc(Jv-R)
OPTION 2 (no uv):    |X| = |R| + min{|Qu|+|Qv| : Qu∈VC(Ju-R), Qv∈VC(Jv-R), Qu∪Qv⊇C}
OPTIMAL = min(Opt1, Opt2).  Test: OPTIMAL <= 2|S|.

--- FINDING ---
  The exchange is a rigid local move. In the PACKING it swaps the rim edges xz,yz
  for the two new spokes hx,hy (new triangle hxy). In the COVER (per E/proof.txt
  Claim 5) it removes the rim edges xz,yz and ADDS the hub-z edges
  Z_z = {uz: uz edge} U {vz: vz edge} — spokes into the kept-aside vertex z, NOT
  into x or y. Blocking comes from the packing side: the new spokes hx,hy must be
  unused by S\{xyz}; in tight certificates those hub spokes are already consumed
  by other packing triangles (edge-disjointness), or do not exist (e.g. u--{8,9},
  v--{6,7}). Hence 204/217 off-hub records are genuinely blocked; only 13 admit a
  single exchange that removes an off-hub triangle (depth 1, no depth-2/3 chains
  observed). In 8 of the changed records the kept-aside vertex z was one-sided
  exclusive, so only one hub-z edge was added to the cover and |X| dropped by 1
  (still valid, now non-tight).
