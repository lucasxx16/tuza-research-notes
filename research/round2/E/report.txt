Main mathematical results:
1. A sufficient two-matchings recipe constructs both packing and cover.
   Its exact hub-edge completion cost gamma is computed from at most
   sixteen subsets of the four-vertex core, including both choices for uv.
   Inequality gamma+t+r <= k+2*epsilon guarantees reducibility.
2. Any local witness can keep its packing and replace all unnecessary
   side-rim deletions by hub spokes; extra rim deletions need only be
   core edges. Each extra core edge repairs at most one budget deficit.
3. A proved triangle-absorption operation reduces hub-free motifs when
   two suitable spokes are unused. It is valid but is not a universal
   simplification algorithm: B found only thirteen saved records improve.
4. A complete human proof settles the 2K2 core. Connected non-WKE links
   force the attachments needed for D's T7, yielding five edge-disjoint
   triangles and a ten-edge cover. Every excluded side configuration is
   ruled out by an explicit WKE matching and vertex cover. The proof
   requires neither the catalogue nor the degree-budget hypothesis.
