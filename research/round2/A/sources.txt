3. F. Botler, C. G. Fernandes, J. Gutiérrez, "On Tuza's conjecture for triangulations and
   graphs with small treewidth", Discrete Math. 344 (4) (2021), 112281;
   doi:10.1016/j.disc.2020.112281; arXiv:2002.07925.
   NOTE: read as quotation inside Gupta's main.tex (lines 204-214), not in the original paper.
   - Lemma 3.3 (irreducible robust graph pair configuration, dg<=6, |N(x)∪N(y)|<=7,
     deg-5/|C|=3/both links K5) and Lemma 3.2(b) (pair non-adjacent), as quoted in
     main.tex lines 204-214. Closest prior structural pair result; does not apply to
     the codegree-four edge case (degree 7, edge not non-edge).
