1. REVIEWED THEOREM.
File: prescribed_edge_cover_k2.txt
Review: C/review_D_full.txt

If nu(G) is one or two, every edge lying in a triangle can be included
in a triangle cover of size at most 2nu(G). This is unconditional and
holds at every order. In particular, in every tight graph with nu=2
and tau=4, each triangle edge belongs to SOME minimum cover. Different
edges may require different covers.

The exact rich three-edge side profile from the previous rounds cannot
occur with packing number k<=2. Any remaining rich side has k>=3.
The proof uses no computation or unproved subgraph-Tuza hypothesis.

2. REVIEWED JOINT DEGREE-CAP BARRIER.
File: joint_caps_barrier.txt
Review: C/review_D_joint_caps.txt

A labeled twelve-vertex graph has mad<8, is robust, satisfies all
conclusions of Puleo Lemma 2.7(a)-(h), and has an independent degree-
seven set whose only link complement is a matching. It is also five-
connected and every nontrivial complete bipartition cut has at least
ten edges. Its degree counts
are n5=3, n6=1, n7=1, n9=4, n10=3. Thus the degree-five/six/seven
deficit is twelve and the degree-nine/ten surplus is ten.

This demonstrates why those exact necessary conditions alone cannot
prove the target-eight average bound. It does not assert irreducibility,
the failure of a previously established local reduction, or a Tuza
counterexample. There are no degree-eight vertices in the example;
in general they have zero initial surplus at target eight.
