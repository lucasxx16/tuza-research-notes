PRESCRIBED-EDGE COVER: LIMITED PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK (round 6)

D proves in research/round6/D/prescribed_edge_cover_k2.txt that when
nu(G) is 1 or 2, every triangle edge belongs to a triangle cover of
size at most 2nu(G). This file does not assess priority or novelty.

Verified from the abstract of Chen, Diao, Hu and Tang,
"Sufficient Conditions for Tuza's Conjecture on Packing and Covering
Triangles," arXiv:1605.01816v2:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.01816v2
If the triangles cover every graph edge, their algorithms find a cover
of size at most 2nu when any of these holds: nu/|T| >= 1/3,
nu/|E| >= 1/4, or |E|/|T| >= 2. The abstract does not establish
the all-graphs nu<=2 prescribed-edge statement. Its full text was not
audited here.

Gupta's source paper describes tau<=2nu for all graphs as Tuza's
conjecture, not a proved universal bound:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06538v1

No complete priority search for D's precise strengthening has been
performed. No novelty claim follows from this limited source check.
