2. PRESCRIBED-EDGE COVERS AT PACKING NUMBER ONE OR TWO
D proves that every triangle edge of a graph with nu in {1,2} lies in a cover
of size at most 2nu. There are no order, degree, connectedness, or conditional
Tuza assumptions. In a tight nu=2 graph, each triangle edge therefore belongs
to SOME minimum cover. This excludes the round-five rich three-edge profile
at k=2, independently of whether the boundary is P4 or a three-edge star.
Together with k=1, a possible rich side must have k>=3. The proof uses the
nu=1 triangle-family classification and complementary triangle/K4 edge deletions,
not the previous atlas experiment. C and coordinator accept it as stated.
Literature priority is not established by the bounded source check.

3. A PRECISE BARRIER TO JOINT COUNTING FROM EXISTING CAPS

Lemma 2.7(a)-(h), and has just one degree-seven vertex with matching-complement

10. Thus these necessary inputs alone cannot yield average degree at least

full symbolic subgraph-density, adjacency, subsumption, connectivity, and cut
arguments. Further interaction exclusions are needed for the mad<8 mainline.
