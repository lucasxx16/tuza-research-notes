ROUND 11 C — D INITIAL MARKED-PSEUDOFOREST REVIEW

The marked-pseudoforest lemma checks under its exact hypotheses. Two old edges of a type-2 triangle cannot carry one original-P label, or its third edge would also be old. Distinct A triangles cannot mark the same P member: two edge-disjoint type-1 pages would replace that one P triangle and augment P. For any component K of the label multigraph built from an edge-disjoint type-2 packing B disjoint from A, replacing its |V(K)| original P triangles with its |E(K)| B pages plus its marked A pages gives a packing of size n−|V(K)|+|E(K)|+marks. Maximality of cardinality of P therefore yields |E(K)|+marks≤|V(K)|. Connectedness makes each component a tree with at most one mark or an unmarked unicyclic multigraph (parallel edges allowed; no loops). The same argument applies separately to each B*/Q book-page choice because such choices are packings disjoint from A.

The proposed a+b+c≤n is NOT proved by this lemma; it combines b book spines and c alternate pages that cannot all be placed simultaneously because paired pages share their red spine. The conditional weighted algebra is correct: 116,48,102,68 times Wang's W1–W4, plus 4(n−a−b−c) and (c−h), cancel a,b,c,d,h and give 334τ≤934n, i.e. 467/167, below 165/59 by 2/9853. This payoff is conditional on the missing global inequality and should not be stated as a theorem. A concrete legal P/A/P'/Q counterexample remains the right attack; a bounded negative scan would not prove the inequality.
