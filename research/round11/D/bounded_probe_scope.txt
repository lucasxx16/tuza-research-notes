ROUND 11 / D / CORRECTED COMPUTATIONAL DIAGNOSTIC SCOPE

The earlier B2 probes reporting only c=0 were invalid. Their residual
construction deleted all E(P') rather than only the old edges B(P').
Those probes therefore did not compute Wang's c. All conclusions
about their sampled c values are withdrawn; they provide no evidence
for either version of a+b+c<=n.

After the residual deletion was corrected, delegated B2 found a
seven-vertex, 17-edge witness with a legal sequence of packing choices
having (n,a,b,m,c)=(4,1,3,3,1). Its exact graph and choices are recorded
in B2/certificate.json. C independently checked that specific chain
in C/review_B2_chain.txt. A separate hand proof of every required
optimum, using degree parity and direct edge inspection, appears in
D/actual_counterexample.txt, independently accepted in
C/review_D_actual_counterexample.txt. No computed optimum is needed.

This positive witness disproves the ALL-LEGAL-CHOICES inequality
  a+b+c<=n.
It does not disprove the existence of some favorable legal choice
in every graph. No counts of favorable or unfavorable choices are
used: the reported aggregate choice enumeration was not part of C's
narrow independent chain audit. No inference about a universal Tuza
constant follows from this counterexample.

The requested scope in D/protocol.txt is a request, not an assertion
of the actual executed scope. Sample counts, elapsed time, cumulative
runs, and completed versus partial searches belong to B2's execution
record and are not independently asserted here. D performed no
numerical enumeration or search. The symbolic results and corrected
positive witness have been reviewed, and D stops this round.
