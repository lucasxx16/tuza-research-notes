ROUND 11 / D
A SEVEN-VERTEX COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE ALL-CHOICES LABEL CANDIDATE

Status: reviewed research proof; see C/review_D_actual_counterexample.txt.
The witness was found by the corrected delegated B2 search and was
independently checked in C/review_B2_chain.txt. The proof below uses
no computed packing optimum, search count, or exhaustive enumeration.

1. DEFINITIONS AND WITNESS.

For a finite simple graph G, let P be a maximum edge-disjoint triangle
packing, n=|P|, and O=E(P). An edge in O is old; every other edge of G
is new. A triangle's type is its number of old edges. Choose a maximum
type-one packing A, put a=|A|, and form G'=G-E(A). Choose P' in G'
to maximize first the number b of its type-two triangles and then
its total size m. Delete only the OLD edges of P' from G'. Let S
be the surviving type-two triangle family, and Q a maximum packing
in S, of size c. These are Wang's packing choices and conventions.

Use vertices {0,1,2,3,4,5,6}, with an edge ij denoting the unordered
pair {i,j}, and triangle ijk denoting its three edges. Let
  E(G)={01,03,04,05,06,13,14,15,16,24,25,26,34,35,36,46,56}.
Take
  P ={016,035,134,256},
  A ={246},
  P'={014,036,135},
  Q ={156}.
We prove that all these choices have their required maximality and
that (n,a,b,m,c)=(4,1,3,3,1). Thus a+b+c=5>4=n.

2. THE ORIGINAL PACKING IS MAXIMUM.

The graph has 17 edges. Vertices 0,1,3,4,5 have degree 5, vertex 2
has degree 3, and vertex 6 has degree 6. In particular, precisely
0,1,2,3,4,5 have odd degree.

The union of any edge-disjoint triangle packing has even degree at
every vertex. Deleting that union therefore leaves a graph in which
these six vertices still have odd degree. Each has remaining degree
at least one, so the remaining graph has at least three edges.
Consequently a packing of size t satisfies 3t<=17-3=14, giving t<=4.
The four triangles of P are present and edge-disjoint, so n=4.

Their old edges are
  O={01,03,05,06,13,14,16,25,26,34,35,56};
the new edges are
  E(G)\O={04,15,24,36,46}.

3. THE TYPE-ONE PACKING IS MAXIMUM.

A type-one triangle has exactly two new edges, which must share a
vertex. The only adjacent pairs among the listed new edges are
  {04,24}, {04,46}, {24,46}, {36,46}.
The first has missing closing edge 02. The other three have old
closing edges 06,26,34 respectively. Hence the entire type-one
family is
  {046,246,346}.
All three use edge 46. Its maximum packing has size one, and A={246}
is a legal maximum choice. Thus a=1.

4. P' IS LEXICOGRAPHICALLY OPTIMAL.

Deleting E(A)={24,26,46} gives
  E(G')={01,03,04,05,06,13,14,15,16,25,34,35,36,56}.
The triangles 014,036,135 are edge-disjoint. Their respective new
edges are 04,36,15, and each has exactly two old edges. Thus P' has
three type-two members and total size three.

Any packing in G' is edge-disjoint from A and extends with A to a
packing in G. Since n=4, every packing in G' has size at most three.
Therefore no packing in G' can have more than three type-two members,
and no packing with three type-two members can have total size more
than three. This proves the required lexicographic optimum (b,m)=(3,3).

5. THE CORRECT RESIDUAL HAS c=1.

The old edges of P' are
  B(P')={01,03,06,13,14,35}.
Deleting these, while retaining the three new edges of P', gives
  E(H)={04,05,15,16,25,34,36,56},
where H=G'-B(P'). Its only triangle is 156.

For a direct check, vertices 0 and 3 have neighbor pairs {4,5} and
{4,6}, whose closing edges 45 and 46 are absent. Vertex 2 has degree
one. Vertex 4 has neighbor pair {0,3}, whose closing edge 03 is absent.
Thus no triangle uses any of 0,2,3,4. The remaining vertices {1,5,6}
induce triangle 156. Its edges 16,56 are old and 15 is new, so it is
type two. Hence S={156}, Q is maximum, and c=1.

6. WHAT THE LABEL GRAPH AND BOOK COST SHOW HERE.

Label the four members of P by
  L1=016, L2=035, L3=134, L4=256.
The unique old edge 26 of A marks L4. The type-two members of P'
give label edges
  014: L1-L3,   036: L1-L2,   135: L2-L3.
Thus the label graph is an unmarked three-cycle on L1,L2,L3 plus
the marked isolated vertex L4. It has zero unmarked tree components,
exactly n-a-b=0, while c=1. This is an actual graph obstruction to
charging every alternate Q-page to a distinct unmarked tree.
It does not contradict the proved marked-component lemma.

Replacing page 135 by Q's page 156 replaces label edge L2-L3 by
L1-L4. The resulting label graph is a tree with its single mark at
L4, again satisfying that lemma.

There is only one paired book: pages 135 and 156 share new spine 15.
The other two vertices are 3 and 6, so its opposite edge is 36.
But 36 belongs to N={04,36,15}, the new edges of P'. Thus the actual
new-opposite-outside-N count from packing_label_constraints.txt is
  k=0.
Since S={156}, its red edge 15 is private and h=c=1. In particular
this example has a+b+k=n. It does not refute a+b+k<=n.

7. EXACT SCOPE AND REMAINING QUESTIONS.

This refutes the assertion that a+b+c<=n holds for EVERY legal
sequence of Wang's packing choices, by exhibiting one
fully legal sequence. It does not show that this graph, or any graph,
lacks some other legal sequence satisfying the inequality. The
version allowing optimized choices remains unresolved by this work.

The alternative inequality a+b+k<=n is also unresolved here, both
in its all-choices and optimized-choice forms. At the previously
recorded algebraic endpoint a=c=h=k=12n/59 and b=36n/59, it would
fail since a+b+k=60n/59. That arithmetic explains its potential
relevance, but does not prove it, realize that endpoint by a graph,
or supply any improved universal constant. No new search is requested.

The earlier B2 probes that reported only c=0 deleted all edges of P'
instead of only B(P'). Those results are invalid for this question.
No aggregate choice counts from the corrected search are used here.
The conditional 467/167 calculation in packing_label_constraints.txt
remains an implication from a universally available favorable choice;
its premise is not proved. No universal improvement of 165/59 follows.
