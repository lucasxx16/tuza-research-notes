ROUTE: ORIGINAL-PACKING LABELS AND REPLACEMENT HALL CONSTRAINTS.
Use Wang's notation P,n,A,a,P',B*,b,Q,c. Label every old edge by its
unique original packed triangle in P. A type-2 triangle uses two
distinct labels: if its two old edges belonged to one original
triangle, its third edge would also be old, contradicting type two.

For any edge-disjoint type-2 packing B disjoint from A, construct a
multigraph L on the n original P-triangles. Its edges are the label
pairs of B. Mark each vertex whose original triangle supplies the
old edge of a member of A. The marks are distinct by maximality of P.

PROVED CANDIDATE LEMMA.
For every component K of L,
  |E(K)| + number_of_marks(K) <= |V(K)|.
Otherwise replace the original P-triangles in K by its B-triangles
and all A-triangles with their old edge labeled in K. Their new
edges avoid P; they are mutually disjoint since A and B are. This
would enlarge P. Thus every component is either a tree with at most
one mark, or an unmarked unicyclic multigraph. Parallel edges are
allowed; loops are not.

When B is obtained by choosing one page from each B*/Q book, the same
property holds for EVERY choice, not merely for B*. The selected
triangles are mutually edge-disjoint and avoid A. Fixed type-three
members of P' can also be retained in the replacement Hall condition,
though the resulting incidence object is no longer just a graph.

PRECISE STRONGER TARGET, NOT YET PROVED.
Can the graph geometry and the simultaneous all-page-choice condition
force, for Wang's construction (or a suitable choice of its maximum
packings),
  a+b+c <= n ?
This is not assumed. It needs adversarial checking and may fail.
The strengthened round-ten equality point has a=c=12n/59 and
b=36n/59, so its sum is 60n/59 and violates the proposed inequality.

EXACT CONDITIONAL PAYOFF.
If the displayed inequality held universally for admissible chosen
packings, Wang's original four cover bounds, weighted respectively
116,48,102,68, together with four times 0<=n-a-b-c and once
0<=c-h, would give
  334 tau(G)<=934 n,
that is tau(G)<=(467/167)nu(G). This is strictly below 165/59,
with gap 2/9853. All a,b,c,d,h coefficients cancel in this sum.
The algebra is conditional; no universal improvement is announced.
