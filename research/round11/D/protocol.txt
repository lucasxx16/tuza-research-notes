ROUND 11 / D / BOUNDED ADVERSARIAL COMPUTATION REQUEST

No code is executed by D. Parent authorizes and delegates the run.
Target: refute the universal-over-all-admissible-choices assertion
  a+b+c<=n
for Wang's original packing sequence. A violating sequence disproves
that assertion, but does not disprove an assertion allowing a specially
optimized choice of the original maximum packing or subsequent ties.

SCOPE.
Start with K5,K6,K7. If time remains, use at most 32 already available
connected triangle-bearing atlas graphs of order at most seven, with
at least twelve edges, in decreasing edge-count order; ties may use
their existing atlas order. Skip duplicate named graphs. No order-eight
generation, new census, or expansion beyond the parent's wall budget.
Stop on the first independently checkable positive witness, or at
120 seconds. A timeout or a finite negative result is not a theorem.
The parent may reduce this scope to fit available compute.

EXACT DEFINITIONS FOR ONE GRAPH G.
1. Enumerate triangles as unordered vertex triples/edge triples.
   A packing is an edge-disjoint subset. Compute n=nu(G) exactly.
   Choose a maximum packing P of size n; write O=union E(P).
2. A triangle's type is its number of edges in O, computed in the
   original G with O fixed for this entire sequence. Compute a as
   the maximum packing number among ALL type-one triangles. Choose
   such a maximum packing A, not merely an inclusion-maximal packing.
3. Delete every edge in E(A), yielding G'. Preserve original edge
   types. All triangles of G' have type two or three; verify this.
4. Choose P' among ALL packings of G' by lexicographically maximizing
     (number of type-two members, total number of members).
   Thus b is first the exact maximum type-two packing number in G'.
   Then m is the exact maximum total packing size subject to exactly
   b type-two members. P' is NOT chosen by maximizing total size first.
   An integer objective (n+1)*b+m is equivalent, since m<=n.
5. Let OldPrime=O intersect E(P'). Delete these OLD edges from G'.
   Define S to be all surviving type-two triangles, equivalently all
   one-new-edge triangles of G'-OldPrime. The new edges of P' are
   NOT deleted in this step. Compute c=nu(S) exactly and select a
   maximum packing Q in S.
6. Test a+b+c>n. When it holds, retain G,P,A,P',Q as the positive
   witness. No upper-bound-only or greedy value may establish this.

CHOICE EXPLORATION.
Within the fixed time budget, alternative maximum P, maximum A, and
lexicographically optimal P' may be explored, since c can depend on
ties. There is no need to exhaust all choices after a positive witness.
If no violation is found, report which graphs and choice counts were
actually completed, and which searches were partial or timed out.

WITNESS OUTPUT FOR VERIFICATION.
Provide an explicit vertex/edge list and all five packings/parameters
G,P,n,A,a,P',b,m,Q,c. Also provide either exact optimization records or
an independent exact recomputation for
  nu(G), nu(type-one family), nu(type-two family of G'),
  max |P'| conditional on b, and nu(S).
The checker must verify edge-disjointness, all type labels, E(A)
deletion, OldPrime deletion, the complete definitions of the families,
and a+b+c>n. It should report a+m<=n as a consistency check.

Optional diagnostic, only after a positive witness: give h, the number
of Q triangles whose new/red edge occurs in exactly one triangle of S;
and d=nu(S after deleting Q's old/blue edges). These are not required
to refute a+b+c<=n and must not delay the primary witness.
