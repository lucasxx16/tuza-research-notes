ACTIVE RESEARCH OBJECTIVE — USER CONFIRMED 2026-10-03

Find an explicit constant c < 165/59 and prove
  tau(G) <= c * nu(G)
for EVERY finite simple graph G.
The user explicitly selected "any finite simple graph" when asked to
distinguish this target from the class mad(G)<8. The decimal 2.797 is
understood as a rounded reference to Wang's exact 165/59, approximately
2.79661016949; a merely smaller rounded decimal is not an improvement.

tau is the minimum number of edges meeting all triangles; nu is the
maximum number of pairwise edge-disjoint triangles. The case nu=0 is
trivial. The desired constant is uniform, independent of graph order.
An asymptotic bound or a bound with an additive term requires a separate
argument to imply the stated uniform result. Numerical searches supply
candidates and counterexamples to proposed lemmas, not universal proofs.
