UNIVERSAL FAMILY.
Two nonadjacent centers share a chosen diamond C=K4-ab. A neighbor p
of the first center outside C sees c,d; two distinct neighbors q,r
of the second center outside C are adjacent. Then the pair is
reducible whenever its degree sum is at most twelve. The vertex p
may equal q or r. The construction uses six triangles, and its cover
size is exactly the degree sum.
